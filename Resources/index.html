Researchers & Authors

  • Denise Hines & Emily Douglas — research on male victims of domestic violence and barriers to help-seeking.
  • Martin Seager & John Barry — work on male psychology, gender bias, and men’s mental health.
  • Warren Farrell — work on boys, fathers, male psychology, and gender expectations.
  • Jaak Panksepp — affective neuroscience, play systems, and emotional development.
  • William Pollack — boys, shame, emotional restriction, and developmental psychology.
  • Shelley Taylor — stress responses, social connection, and emotional regulation.
  • William Frey — crying research and emotional tears.
  • Kurt Gray & Daniel Wegner — moral typecasting and perceptions of agency and victimhood.
